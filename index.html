Craftsmanship & Atmosphere

"Antiquity Cafe was born from a passion for single-origin heirloom coffee roasting, artisanal baking, bronze-cut gourmet plates, and warm hospitality."

Every bean served in our cafe is harvested from sustainable high-altitude micro-lots in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala. Roasted in small batches, our espresso extractions bring out floral aromatics, natural sweetness, and rich crema.
